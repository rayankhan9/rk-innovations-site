Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Applies to the CompetePrep app and this page

Who we are

CompetePrep is developed and operated by RK Innovations ("RK Innovations", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information CompetePrep collects, why, and how it's handled.

Information we do not collect

CompetePrep has no sign-up, login, or user account of any kind. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other way to identify you personally.

Information stored on your device

When you practice questions, take mock tests, or bookmark a question, that activity is saved in a local database on your own phone, so the app can show your progress and let you resume where you left off. This information:

  • never leaves your device or gets sent to us or any server we control;
  • is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app, or on demand using the in-app "Reset progress" option on any paper;
  • is not linked to any personal identity, because the app has none to link it to.

Advertising

CompetePrep shows ads through Google AdMob to keep the app free. AdMob may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general location, and app interaction data, in order to serve and measure ads. This happens under Google's own privacy policy, not ours:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data when you use AdMob-supported apps

Ads are never shown inside a timed mock test — only in practice mode and between screens. Depending on your region, you may be able to limit ad personalization through your device's ad settings (for example, Android Settings → Privacy → Ads).

We do not run our own analytics or tracking SDKs beyond what AdMob itself includes.

Data retention and deletion

Since your practice data lives only on your device, uninstalling CompetePrep permanently deletes it. There is nothing stored on our end to separately request deletion of.

Children's privacy

CompetePrep is built for students preparing for JEE, NEET, GATE, and CLAT — generally aged 15 and older — and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

Because CompetePrep has no accounts and stores practice data only on-device, there's no server-side account database to secure or breach. Ad-related traffic between the app and Google's AdMob servers is encrypted (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or the app — write to privacy@rk-innovations.in.
