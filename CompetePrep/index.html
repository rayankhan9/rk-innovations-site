How it works

Coverage

Built from real past papers.

Every answer is checked against the official answer key from NTA, the IITs, the GATE organising institutes, or the Consortium of NLUs. Where an exam body doesn't publish the question booklet itself (as with NEET 2025 and 2026), the questions come from a reproduction of the paper, while the answers still come from the official key.
