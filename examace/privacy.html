Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Applies to the ExamAce app and this page

Who we are

ExamAce is developed and operated by RK Innovations ("RK Innovations", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information ExamAce collects, why, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password when you register. We never store your password in plain text.
  • Practice activity — the questions you attempt, the answers you submit, your scores, and the AI-generated feedback for each attempt, so your progress dashboard can be built from it.
  • Plan status — whether your account is on the Free tier or has an active Premium pass, and when that pass expires.
  • Basic technical data — standard request metadata (like IP address) used only for rate-limiting and abuse prevention, not for tracking.

We do not run advertising and do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs in the app.

How your answers are graded

When you submit a practice answer, its text is sent to Anthropic (the maker of Claude) solely to generate a score, feedback, and a model answer. Anthropic processes this text as our service provider under its own data-handling terms and does not use it to train models. No other content from your account is shared with Anthropic.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and keep you signed in.
  • To grade your practice answers and show you feedback.
  • To calculate your progress and topic-mastery dashboard.
  • To enforce the free-tier daily question limit and Premium entitlements.
  • To investigate abuse — such as automated sign-in attempts — using rate-limiting.

We do not sell your personal information, to anyone, ever.

Payments

Premium access, once live, is sold as a one-time purchase through Google Play's billing system, covering 1 year of access. It is not a recurring subscription — there is no auto-renewal, and we will never charge you again without you actively choosing to buy another year. RK Innovations does not directly receive or store your payment card details; Google Play handles that.

Data retention

We keep your account and practice history for as long as your account is active, so your progress isn't lost between sessions.

Delete your account

To delete your ExamAce account, email privacy@rk-innovations.in from the address your account is registered under, with the subject line "Delete my account." We don't yet have a self-service delete option in the app, but we'll confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Deleting your account removes:

  • Your name, email address, and password
  • Your practice history — every question attempted, answer submitted, and score
  • Your Premium plan status and purchase history

This can't be undone. There's currently no partial-deletion option — an account-deletion request removes all of the above together, not select pieces of it.

Children's privacy

ExamAce is built for students preparing for CBSE Class 8–12 exams, many of whom are minors. We collect only what's described above, never request more than a name and email to create an account, and recommend that students under 18 have a parent or guardian review this policy with them before signing up.

Security

Passwords are hashed, not stored in plain text; all traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS); and sign-in is protected against automated password-guessing with rate limits and account lockouts.

Your rights

You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account entirely, at any time, by contacting us below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be noted in the app.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data — write to privacy@rk-innovations.in.
