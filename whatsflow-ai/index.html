How it works

Live on the number your customers already use.

No new app for customers to install. They keep messaging you on WhatsApp, and WhatsFlow AI works behind the scenes.

1

Connect your WhatsApp number

Link your WhatsApp Business number through Meta's Cloud API in your account settings. Customers keep messaging the number they already know.

2

Teach it your business

Upload FAQs, policies and product notes as pasted text or .txt / .md files. They're indexed so the AI can find the right passage for each question.

3

Replies go out, humans stay in charge

Confident answers are sent straight away. Anything outside your documents is escalated to your team instead of being guessed at.

Questions

Good to know.

Do my customers need to install anything?

No. They keep chatting with you on WhatsApp exactly as they do today. Your team uses the WhatsFlow AI dashboard in a web browser.

What if the AI doesn't know the answer?

It doesn't guess. If the answer isn't covered by your uploaded documents, the conversation is escalated to your team's inbox with the full context, so a person can pick it up.

What do I need to get started?

A WhatsApp Business number connected to Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API, and the FAQs or policies you want the assistant to answer from. We'll walk you through the rest.

Which AI does it use?

Replies are drafted by Anthropic's Claude, using the recent conversation and the passages retrieved from your knowledge base. Document search uses embeddings from OpenAI. Our privacy policy lists every service involved.

Is our data kept separate from other businesses?

Yes. Every business account has its own conversations, WhatsApp credentials and knowledge base, and one account can't see another's.
