## 1. Positioning

**One line:** Attendance that only counts where it should.
**Alt taglines:** *Sign in where you belong.* · *Geofenced attendance, verified by the server.* · *Draw the boundary. Attendance does the rest.*

**Elevator pitch (30 s):**
AttendancePro is geofenced attendance for companies, schools and campuses. You draw the allowed area on a map, people sign in and out from their Android phone only while they're inside it, and the server verifies every punch. Members see their own statistics, admins get live dashboards, and everyone gets a monthly report automatically. No hardware, no paper registers.

**Three pillars**
1. **Trustworthy records** — location, accuracy, device and clock are checked on the server; suspicious punches are flagged for review.
2. **Effortless for people** — one tap, a clear map, personal stats, leave and corrections in the app.
3. **Control for admins** — live dashboard, shifts, holidays, approvals, monthly reports, audit log. Runs on *your* server.

**Audiences & what each cares about**
| Audience | Pain | Lead with |
|---|---|---|
| School / college principals, registrars | Staff late-coming, paper registers, manual monthly summaries | Campus boundary, department views, monthly report |
| HR / admin managers (SMEs, branches) | Buddy punching, disputes, payroll prep | Server-verified punches, flagged review, CSV export |
| Clinics, retail, facilities, field teams | Many sites, shift workers | Multiple locations, shifts, offline sign-in |
| Staff / members | "Did my attendance get recorded?" | Live status, personal stats, history |

**Tone:** clear, practical, a little warm. Concrete beats clever. No hype words ("revolutionary", "AI-powered"). No fear-mongering about employees.

## 2. Claims guardrails (keep marketing truthful)
**Say:** "verified on the server", "flags suspicious punches for review", "detects mock-location use on Android", "one phone per account (optional)", "works with poor signal — punches sync later", "no background tracking", "runs on your own server", "maps by OpenStreetMap".
**Don't say:** "tamper-proof", "100% accurate", "prevents all cheating", "GDPR/DPDP compliant" (compliance depends on how each organization runs it), "free" or any price, "AI", "works on iPhone" (Android member app only today), numbers of customers/users (none yet).
**Always:** show screenshots as *sample data*; keep "Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors" visible where maps appear.
