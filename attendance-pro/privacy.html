Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Applies to the AttendancePro Android app and this page

Who we are, and who is responsible for your data

AttendancePro is software developed by RK Innovations ("RK Innovations", "we", "us"). The app is used through your organization — your employer, school, college or other organization (the "Organization"). The Organization decides who may use AttendancePro, runs the server that stores attendance records, and is responsible for how that data is used. This policy explains what the app collects and where it goes.

Information the app collects

When you register and use the app, the following is sent to your Organization's AttendancePro server:

  • Account details: your name, email address, password (stored only as a salted hash), and — if you provide them — your ID or roll number, department and designation.
  • Attendance records: the time, your precise location (latitude, longitude and GPS accuracy) and the matched location name each time you sign in or sign out, plus whether the punch was made offline.
  • Device information: a random identifier generated when you install the app and your phone's make and model. The identifier is not your Android advertising ID. If your Organization enables device binding, it is used to link your account to one phone.
  • Selfie (only if your Organization requires it): a photo taken with the camera at the moment you sign in or out.
  • Requests you make: leave and attendance-correction requests, including the reasons you type.

When your location is read

The app reads your location only while it is open on your screen — to show on the map whether you are inside an allowed area — and at the moment you sign in or sign out. The live position shown on the map stays on your phone. It is not sent to the server and is not recorded unless you sign in or out. The app does not request background location access and does not track you when it is closed.

How the information is used

  • to verify that you are inside an allowed area when you sign in or out, and to record attendance;
  • to show you your own history and statistics;
  • to let your Organization's administrators review attendance, approve leave and corrections, flag unusual punches, and produce reports;
  • to email you a monthly attendance summary, using the email address you registered with;
  • to keep the service secure (for example, rate limiting sign-in attempts and an audit log of administrator actions).

We do not sell your information, and the app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Who can see your information

  • Your Organization's administrators can see your account details, attendance records, locations of your punches, requests and any selfies.
  • You can see your own records and statistics in the app.
  • Other members cannot see your information.
  • RK Innovations does not have access to your Organization's records unless the Organization has asked us to host or support its AttendancePro server.

Third-party services

To display maps, the app requests map tiles from the OpenStreetMap tile servers. Like any web request, this shares your device's IP address and the map area being viewed with the OpenStreetMap Foundation, under its privacy policy. No account details or attendance data are included in these requests.

Monthly reports and password-reset codes are sent by email through the mail service configured by your Organization.

Storage, retention and deletion

Records are stored on your Organization's server, which determines how long they are kept. Selfies, if used, are stored privately on that server and are visible only to administrators. On your phone, the app keeps a sign-in session token in the device's secure storage and a small cache of your locations and any punches waiting to be synced; uninstalling the app removes them. To access, correct or delete your attendance data, contact your Organization's administrator — they control the records and can delete your account and its history. You can also write to us at privacy@rk-innovations.in and we will direct your request to the right Organization.

Security

Data is sent between the app and the server over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as salted hashes, sign-in sessions use short-lived tokens, and attempts to sign in repeatedly are limited. No system is perfectly secure, and the Organization is responsible for the security of the server it runs.

Children's privacy

AttendancePro is intended for use by employees, staff and students as arranged by an Organization. Where an Organization registers minors, it is responsible for obtaining any required consent from parents or guardians. We do not knowingly collect information from children outside such an arrangement.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Location (precise): to verify you are inside an allowed area, as described above.
  • Camera: only if your Organization requires a selfie with each punch.
  • Network access: to talk to your Organization's server and load maps.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or the app — write to privacy@rk-innovations.in.
